## House Manual

**Title:** HomeRecord House Manual [23]

**Short description:** The owner's manual your house never came with. Breakers, shutoffs, warranties. [78]

**Full description:**

House Manual keeps what you know about your house in one record tied to a plan of the house, so anyone at home can find the water main, the right breaker or the furnace filter size in a hurry.

Draw a rough floor plan, room by room and floor by floor. Tap a room to see what's in it, or open it in 3D with your own wall photos on the walls.

Add the things in your house: water heater, furnace, AC, sump pump, appliances, garage door opener, roof, deck. Scan the data plate and the app reads the make, model and serial. It looks the warranty up once, shows where it found it, and keeps it with the item.

Map the breaker panel slot by slot. Plug the phone into an outlet, flip breakers, and mapping mode sounds an alarm when that outlet goes dead, so one person can label the whole panel. Print the legend for the panel door.

Emergency shows every shutoff with a photo and how to close it, in big type, offline. Open it from Home, a quick settings tile or a home screen widget.

Mark where pipes, wires, ducts and gas lines run behind the walls before the drywall goes up, on the wall photo or on the plan.

Reminders tell you when filters, seasonal jobs and warranties come due. The home report PDF lists every room and item for an insurer, a buyer or a house sitter.

If your contractor uses Field Log, they can hand the house to you at the end of the job and you start with it filled in.

No account needed. Everything stays on your phone and works with no signal. Make an encrypted backup file any time. Pro adds more items and panels, reminders, the home report, cloud sync between phones and sharing with family. Annual or lifetime, your call.

## Field Log

**Title:** HomeRecord Field Log [20]

**Short description:** Time clock, photos, daily logs and change orders for a small crew. [66]

**Full description:**

Field Log runs the jobsite for a contractor with a crew of 1 to 10. Your crew clocks in on their phones, takes the photos and notes, and the paperwork writes itself from the day.

Time clock with GPS. Clock in, take a break, switch jobs, clock out. If someone punches far from the job the app asks why. A lead can clock in the whole crew with one tap.

Plans and files on every phone, offline in the basement. Pinch to zoom and mark up a page.

Photos tagged to the job, room and phase, with before and after. Volume key shutter for gloves. Notes and voice memos.

The daily log puts together who was on site and for how long, what got done, photos, weather, materials and delays. The lead reviews it and sends a PDF to the client and the office.

Tasks and a punch list per job, pinned to a room. Change orders priced on the spot and signed by the client on the phone, or sent as a link.

The owner sees who is clocked in and where, approves weekly timesheets with the odd punches flagged, and exports hours to CSV or a QuickBooks timesheet file.

Before drywall, photograph each wall and mark where the pipes and wires went. At close out, hand the house to the homeowner. They open it in House Manual with everything filled in.

Works with no signal and syncs when it can. 14 day free trial, then Solo for one person or Crew for up to ten.

**Screenshots (tour shots):** today, jobs, clock, schedule, plus a daily log, a change order being signed and the timesheet from the walkthrough flows.

**Category:** Business. **Contact email** and **privacy policy URL:** as for House Manual.
