# HomeRecord privacy policy (draft, needs a review and a contact address before publishing)

Effective: <date of first release>

HomeRecord consists of two Android apps, **House Manual** and **Field Log**. This policy covers both.

## Your data stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. That covers your panel, breakers, rooms, shutoffs, equipment, reminders, jobs, photos, notes and voice memos. We do not run servers that receive it. The apps have no analytics, advertising or crash reporting code.

## What leaves the device

- **Only when you choose.** If you export a PDF, a handoff, a `.homerecord` file or a backup and share it, the file goes to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. We never see it. A backup is encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.
- **Purchases (House Manual only).** If you buy House Manual Pro, Google Play processes the payment. RevenueCat, our purchase service provider, receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt so it can unlock Pro. No names, emails, photos or home data are sent. RevenueCat and Google each have their own privacy policy for that data.

## Permissions

- Camera: take photos of panels, shutoffs, equipment labels and job sites. Text on equipment labels is read on the device. No photo is uploaded.
- Microphone (Field Log): record voice memos you start.
- Notifications (House Manual): show the reminders you set up.
- Vibration (House Manual): feedback in Emergency mode.

## Children

The apps are not meant for children. They collect no personal information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling an app deletes its data. Files you exported and shared stay wherever you sent them.
