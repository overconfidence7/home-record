# HomeRecord Privacy Policy (draft: have it reviewed and add a contact address before publishing)

Effective: <date of first release>

HomeRecord consists of two Android apps, **House Manual** and **Field Log**. This policy covers both.

## Your data stays on your phone

Everything you enter (your electrical panel, breakers, rooms, shutoffs, equipment, maintenance reminders, jobs, daily photos, notes and voice recordings) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not run servers that receive it, and the apps contain no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

## What leaves the device

- **Only when you choose.** If you export a PDF, a `.homerecord` file or a backup and share it, the file goes to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. We never see it. A backup can be protected with a passphrase you choose; we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.
- **Purchases (House Manual only).** If you buy House Manual Pro, Google Play processes the payment and RevenueCat, our subscription service provider, receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt to unlock Pro. No names, emails, photos or home data are sent. See RevenueCat's and Google's privacy policies for how they handle that data.

## Permissions

- Camera: take photos of panels, shutoffs, equipment labels and job sites. Text on equipment labels is read on the device; no image is uploaded.
- Microphone (Field Log): record voice notes you start.
- Notifications (House Manual): show maintenance reminders you create.
- Vibration (House Manual): feedback in Emergency Mode.

## Children

The apps are not directed to children and collect no personal information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling an app deletes its data. Photos or files you exported and shared remain wherever you sent them.
